
## **INT304: Network Security and Protocols – Lab 2: Network Security Policies and Risk Management**

### **Objectives**

* Understand the importance of network security policies.
* Learn how to conduct a risk assessment.
* Develop skills to create effective security policies for an organization.
* Develop collaborative skills and document group discussions for reporting.

### **Lab Activities**

1. **Introduction to Network Security Policies**

   * Discuss the key components of an effective security policy, including:

     * **Purpose and Scope**: What the policy aims to protect.
     * **User Responsibilities**: Expectations and acceptable use of network resources.
     * **Incident Response Plan**: Steps to take when a security breach occurs.
     * **Compliance**: Adherence to relevant laws and regulations.

2. **Conducting a Risk Assessment**

   * Form groups of **5 students per group**.
   * Each group selects a hypothetical organization (e.g., healthcare provider, financial institution).
   * Identify potential threats and vulnerabilities specific to your organization.
   * Conduct a risk assessment using the following steps:

     * **Asset Identification**: List key assets requiring protection (e.g., customer data, intellectual property).
     * **Threat Identification**: Identify potential threats to each asset (e.g., cyberattacks, natural disasters).
     * **Vulnerability Assessment**: Analyze weaknesses that could be exploited by threats.
     * **Impact Analysis**: Evaluate the potential impact of each threat.
     * **Risk Rating**: Assign a risk rating (High, Medium, Low) based on likelihood and impact.

3. **Developing Security Policies**

   * Using the risk assessment findings, each group drafts a preliminary security policy for their organization. Include:

     * **Access Control Policies**: Guidelines for user access and authentication.
     * **Data Protection Policies**: Measures to safeguard sensitive information.
     * **Incident Response Procedures**: Steps to be taken during a security incident.
     * **Training and Awareness Programs**: Plans for educating employees on security best practices.

4. **Recording Group Discussion**

   * During the development of your security policy, **record your group discussion** using any video recording tool (phone, laptop camera, Zoom, Google Meet, etc.).
   * Ensure **all group members participate** in the discussion.

5. **Presentation of Policies**

   * Each group presents their risk assessment and proposed security policy to the class.
   * Engage in peer feedback and discuss the strengths and weaknesses of each policy.

6. **Reflection and Discussion**

   * Participate in a class discussion on adapting security policies to evolving threats and organizational needs.
   * Discuss how organizations can regularly review and update policies to remain effective.
